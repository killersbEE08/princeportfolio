Product analytics for a live web product, owned end to end.

## What it does

- A GA4 measurement plan designed from scratch — custom events, goals, and dimensions tracking the full user journey from acquisition through retention
- Funnel drop-off and retention analysis used to iterate on site architecture and content, improving session depth and return-visitor rate
- Search-trend and keyword-demand analysis to prioritize the content roadmap, with channel CAC compared via cost-benefit analysis to guide budget

## Why it matters

Most analytics setups measure vanity metrics. This one was built to answer product questions and directly shape what got built next.
