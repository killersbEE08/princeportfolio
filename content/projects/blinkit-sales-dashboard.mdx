A complete Power BI solution built to answer one question: **where is our revenue really coming from, and what should we do about it?**

## What it does

- Interactive dashboard analyzing sales performance across product categories, outlet types, and geographic locations
- DAX measures and a clean data model tracking revenue trends, outlet performance, and item-level insights
- Category-level views that support strategic, data-driven merchandising decisions

## Approach

I modeled the raw sales data, wrote reusable DAX measures for revenue and performance KPIs, and designed the report around the decisions stakeholders actually needed to make — not just the charts that looked good.
