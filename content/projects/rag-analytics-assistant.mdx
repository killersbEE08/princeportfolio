An experiment in making analytics **conversational** — ask a question in plain English, get a source-cited answer.

## What it does

- Retrieval-augmented generation over business reports and structured data
- Natural-language questions mapped to SQL and document retrieval
- Source-cited answers so every number can be traced back to its origin

## Why I built it

Dashboards answer the questions you anticipated. I wanted to explore how RAG and LLMs can answer the ones you didn't — while keeping the rigor and traceability analytics demands.
