title: "What a Blinkit sales dashboard taught me about asking better questions"
description: "Building a Power BI dashboard is easy. Building one that changes a decision is the hard part."
date: "2025-06-01"
---

When I built the Blinkit Sales Performance Dashboard, my first version was packed with charts. Revenue by category, sales by outlet, trends over time — all technically correct, and almost none of it useful.

## The problem wasn't the data

The problem was that I started with the charts I *could* build instead of the decisions the business needed to make. A dashboard full of accurate visuals can still fail if nobody knows what to do after looking at it.

## What changed

I rebuilt it around three questions:

- **Where is revenue concentrated** across categories and outlet types?
- **Which outlets are underperforming** relative to similar ones?
- **What item-level patterns** should drive the next merchandising decision?

Everything that didn't help answer one of those questions came off the canvas. The DAX measures got simpler. The story got clearer.

## The takeaway

Good analytics isn't about showing everything you measured. It's about answering the question someone is actually trying to make a decision on — and making that answer impossible to miss.
